[0047]The disclosure also provides, among other things, a polypeptide that binds human
thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) and which is designed by a method comprising
generating a polypeptide sequence with a CBO model. In some embodiments, the polypeptide
is further designed by verifying the generated polypeptide sequence using the CBO model by,
for each amino acid position of the polypeptide sequence, calculating a plurality of energy
scores. The energy scores can be based on having substituted the amino acid at a given
position in the polypeptide sequence with each of a plurality of different amino acids. In
some embodiments, the polypeptide is further verified by adding each energy score
calculated to an array. In some embodiments, the polypeptide is further verified by generating
a normalized sum of the energy scores for each position. In some embodiments, the
polypeptide is further verified by calculating a logarithm of each energy score calculated. In
some embodiments, the polypeptide is further verified by generating a score of the
polypeptide sequence by summing the logarithms of each energy score calculated. The score
represents a functional property of the polypeptide's ability to bind to human TSLP. In some
embodiments, the polypeptide sequence is verified if the polypeptide scores above a
particular threshold.
