[00517] In some embodiments, a polypeptide has a lower poly-specificity for a non-
specific target (e.g., DNA) than the Reference Antibody. A DNA polyspecificity reagent
(PSR) score is a measure of antibody binding to a non-specific DNA target. A DNA PSR
score is calculated by dividing an ELISA or DELFIA score by a background score of a
secondary antibody alone. A lower DNA PSR score, indicative of a lower poly-specific
binding activity, is preferred for a polypeptide of the invention.
