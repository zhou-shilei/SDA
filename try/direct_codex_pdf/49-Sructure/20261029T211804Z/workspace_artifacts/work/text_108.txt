Fusion Proteins
[00527] The disclosure also provides, among other things, a fusion protein comprising one
or more of the polypeptides disclosed herein.
[00528]The term “fusion protein" refers to a synthetic, semi-synthetic or recombinant
single protein molecule. A fusion protein can comprise all or a portion of two or more
different proteins and/or polypeptides that are attached by covalent bonds (e.g., peptide
bonds).
