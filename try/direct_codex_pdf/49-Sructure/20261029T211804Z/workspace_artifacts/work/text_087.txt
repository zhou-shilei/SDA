Antibodies and Antigen Binding Fragments
[00410] In some embodiments, a polypeptide is an immunoglobulin molecule, such as an
antibody (e.g., a whole antibody, an intact antibody) or an antigen-binding fragment of an
antibody.
[00411] In some embodiments, a polypeptide is an antibody. As used herein, the term
“antibody"refers to an immunoglobulin molecule capable of specific binding to a target, such
as a carbohydrate, polynucleotide, lipid, polypeptide, etc., through at least one antigen
recognition site, located in the variable domain of the immunoglobulin molecule. As used
herein, the term “antibody"refers to a full-length antibody.
