[00522] In some embodiments, a polypeptide has a weaker self-association than the
Reference Antibody, for example, as determined by an affinity-capture self-interaction
nanoparticle spectroscopy (AC-SINS) value. The AC-SINS value is the change in maximum
absorbance wavelength in the coated-nanoparticle absorption spectra compared to the spectra
of the nanoparticle alone. Thus, the greater the change in maximum absorbance wavelength,
the more self-interaction of the antibody coated on the nanopartical. Self-association is an
unwanted property that correlates with poor viscosity and poor PK properties. Techniques
and assays for assessing self-association of proteins are known in the art. See, e.g., Patro &
Przybycien, Biotechnol Bioeng. 52(2):193-203 (1996), the contents of which are incorporated
herein in their entirety.
