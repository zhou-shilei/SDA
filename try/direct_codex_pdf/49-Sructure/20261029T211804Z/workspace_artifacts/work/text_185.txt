Example 2. Polyspecific Reactivity of Candidate TSLP Binders
[00765]Non-specific interaction, or poly-specific reactivity (PSR), measures the potential
for an antibody to bind non-specifically to things other than its desired target, in this case,
TSLP. PSR was measured with using a low-stringency ELISA-based method (Tiller et al., J
Immunol Methods. 329:112-24 (2008)). Briefly, a 384-well assay plate was coated with 10
μg/mL salmon sperm DNA (Rockland Immunochemicals, Inc., Gilbertsville, PA) and
incubated overnight at 4°C. All antigens were diluted in Tris-buffered saline (TBS). The
assay plate was washed 3 times with 100 μL TBS-Tween-20 (TBS-T) on an automated plate
washer and blocked with 100 μL of TBS-T with 5% bovine serum albumin (BSA) for one
hour at 37ºC. The assay plate was washed 3 times as before. The purified full-length IgG
molecules described in Example 1 were normalized to 100 nM in TBS-T and distributed in
duplicate. The plate was incubated for 1 hour at 37ºC and again washed 3 times as before.
DELFIA®(dissociation-enhanced lanthanide fluorescence immunoassay) Eu-N1 anti-human
IgG secondary (PerkinElmer Inc., Waltham, MA) was diluted to 100 ng/mL in TBS-T plus
5% BSA, and 25 μL was distributed into each well. The plate was incubated for 1 hour at
37°C then washed 3 time as before. 25 μL of DELFIA®Enhancement Solution (PerkinElmer
Inc.) was added to each well and incubated in the dark for 15 minutes. Time Resolved
