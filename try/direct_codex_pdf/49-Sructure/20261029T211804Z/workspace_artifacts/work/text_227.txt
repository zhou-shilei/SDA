A polypeptide:

47.
A polypeptide that binds human thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP), wherein the
polypeptide is designed by a method comprising:
generating a polypeptide sequence with a CBO model;
verifying the generated polypeptide sequence using the CBO model by:
for each amino acid position of the polypeptide sequence, calculating a
plurality of energy scores, the energy scores based on having substituted the
amino acid at a given position in the polypeptide sequence with each of a
plurality of different amino acids,
adding each energy score calculated to an array,
generating a normalized sum of the energy scores for each position,
calculating a logarithm of each energy score calculated, and
generating a score of the polypeptide sequence by summing the
logarithms of each energy score calculated, the score representing a functional
property of the polypeptide's ability to bind to human TSLP.
